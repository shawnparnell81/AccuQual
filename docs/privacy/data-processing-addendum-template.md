# Data Processing Addendum — TEMPLATE

> **This is a starting template, not legal advice.** Have a lawyer review and adapt it before use. Text in
> **[brackets]** must be decided or filled in. It is written to be attached to a customer subscription agreement
> and is deliberately generic: it does not assume any particular privacy law applies.
>
> This addendum does not claim, and must not be read to claim, that AccuQual is certified by ISO, IATF or any other
> standards body.

**Between:** **[Customer legal name]** ("Customer") and **[AccuQual legal entity]** ("AccuQual").
**Effective date:** **[date]**. This addendum forms part of the agreement between them (the "Agreement").

## 1. Definitions

"Customer Data" means data the Customer or its users enter into or upload to the service, including records about
the Customer's own suppliers, customers, employees and products. "Personal Data" means Customer Data that relates to
an identifiable person. "Subprocessor" means a third party AccuQual engages to process Customer Data.

## 2. Roles

The Customer decides what Customer Data is put into the service and why; AccuQual processes it only to provide the
service and on the Customer's documented instructions. The Agreement and the Customer's use of the service's
features are those instructions.

## 3. What AccuQual does with Customer Data

- Stores, retrieves, displays and transmits it as the Customer's users direct.
- Backs it up and keeps it available and secure.
- Sends emails and notifications the Customer's configuration or users trigger.
- Where the Customer uses AI features, sends the relevant text to the AI provider to obtain the result.
- Does **not** sell Customer Data, use it to train models, or use it for advertising. **[confirm this matches practice and any AI provider terms]**

## 4. Confidentiality

AccuQual limits access to Customer Data to personnel who need it to run and support the service, and who are bound
by confidentiality obligations.

## 5. Security

AccuQual maintains the measures described in **Annex A** (see `data-handling-summary.md`), and will not materially
reduce them during the term. The Customer is responsible for its own accounts: choosing who has access, keeping
credentials secret, and using the two-step sign-in and single sign-on options the service provides.

## 6. Subprocessors

The Customer authorizes the Subprocessors listed in **Annex B** (see `subprocessors.md`). AccuQual will give the
Customer **[30]** days' notice of a new or replacement Subprocessor that handles Customer Data; the Customer may
object on reasonable grounds within that time, and the parties will discuss in good faith. AccuQual remains
responsible for its Subprocessors' handling of Customer Data under this addendum.

## 7. Incidents

AccuQual will tell the Customer without undue delay, and within **[72]** hours, after becoming aware of a security
incident that led to unauthorized access to, or loss or disclosure of, Customer Data, and will provide what
information it has to help the Customer respond. AccuQual will take reasonable steps to contain and remediate it.

## 8. Assistance

Taking into account the nature of the service, AccuQual will reasonably help the Customer respond to requests from
people about their Personal Data. The service lets the Customer's administrators export all Customer Data at any
time (Admin Console → Data Export) and correct or deactivate records themselves. AccuQual will help with anything
further **[at no charge for reasonable requests / at its then-current rates]**.

## 9. Return and deletion

- **During the term:** the Customer can export its data at any time using the built-in export.
- **On termination or expiry:** the Customer has **[30]** days to export. After that, and within **[30]** further
  days of the Customer's written request, AccuQual will permanently delete the Customer's Customer Data from the
  live service. Copies in backups are removed as those backups expire, within **[confirm backup retention]**.
- AccuQual may keep what the law requires it to keep, and will keep it confidential and use it only for that purpose.
- Records of who changed what (the audit trail) form part of Customer Data and are deleted with it.

## 10. Audits and information

AccuQual will make available information reasonably needed to show it is meeting this addendum, including
`data-handling-summary.md`, and will answer the Customer's reasonable written security questionnaires **[once a year]**.
On-site audits **[are not offered / are available at the Customer's cost, with reasonable notice and subject to confidentiality]**.

## 11. Location of processing

Customer Data is hosted in **[region(s) — see Annex B]**. AccuQual will not move stored Customer Data outside
**[region]** without notice under section 6.

## 12. Liability and order of precedence

Liability under this addendum is subject to the limits in the Agreement **[confirm with counsel]**. If this addendum
and the Agreement conflict about Customer Data, this addendum applies.
